Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Universall Balance ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in the Little Graphic Designer mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Our app is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal user data. The app operates as a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) and all creative work stays entirely on your device.

2. Local Storage & Permissions

Little Graphic Designer may request permissions to access your device storage to allow you to save or export your designs. This data is handled locally by your operating system and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. Children's Privacy

Little Graphic Designer is a tool for creators of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, the app is safe for children to use. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from anyone.

4. Third-Party Services

This app is hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic server logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes, but we do not have access to this data for marketing or tracking.
